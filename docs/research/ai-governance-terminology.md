# AI Governance Terminology: Official Vendor Research

Research date: 10 October 2026. Research only; no application changes.

## Evidence boundaries

Only official vendor documentation and product materials support the claims below. Product pages describe capabilities; they do not necessarily establish exact application sidebar labels. Documentation contents menus and website navigation are not application navigation. **Unverified** means the reviewed public sources did not establish the requested detail, not that the feature is absent. Labels vary by release, profile, licensing and configuration. No authenticated vendor application was inspected.

## Common patterns and VendorGuard recommendation

The sources above support a mixed vocabulary: explicit AI context for inventories and governed records, ordinary governance terms inside that context, and assessment names that reflect the actual method or purpose. There is no verified universal sidebar naming standard shared by all eight vendors. These are research conclusions, not claims that every vendor uses identical labels.

For VendorGuard, retain the existing sidebar structure and use **AI Risk Assessments** and **AI Impact Assessments** alongside **AI Inventory** and **AI Use Cases**. Keep familiar names such as Findings, Remediation, Evidence, Controls and Risk Acceptance where their surrounding context is clear. Explain the AI-system subject on assessment pages, and keep third-party/vendor assessments distinct. A mixed work queue should use **Subject**, while AI-system-only lists can use **AI System**.

Do not infer live model monitoring, certification or automated compliance from competitors' terminology. Only name source frameworks when supported by VendorGuard's implemented method. Preserve independent human review and the principle: **AI assists. Humans govern.**
